# Final demo script: Squat Tutor: Coach's Eye (target 2:45, limit < 3:00)

**Applies to branch `feature/coach-eye`.** If the owner keeps `main` (no mistake comparison), use `DEMO_SCRIPT.md` instead.
The footage is a **Lens Studio preview recording** (SPECS 27, Interactive), not footage through glasses.
Setup, framing, and checklists: `FINAL_DEMO_RECORDING_GUIDE.md`.

| Time | Do | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:18** | Hold on the opening screen | Intro card, figure on the stage | "Is this squat good? From one camera angle you often can't tell. Trainers check the knees from the front and the heels and chest from the side. A single video only gives you one of those views." |
| **0:18–0:32** | Move the cursor over the card, then the figure | Same | "Squat Tutor: Coach's Eye is a SPECS Lens, shown in the Lens Studio preview. | **0:32–0:48** | **Start**; let the full squat play (about 12 s) | The card follows phases 1→5; the completion ring appears | "Start plays the whole movement: stance, a controlled descent, the bottom, rising, and finishing tall. Each phase has one short cue and one highlight on the body." |

| **1:05–1:22** | **Heels** | Stage turns to Side; red shins push forward and heels lift | "Heels lifting is a side-view problem. The red figure's heels come up and the knees shoot forward. The white figure keeps the whole foot planted." |

| **2:35–2:50** | **Slow 0.5x** (off), **Replay** | Squat restarts | "Squat Tutor: Coach's Eye shows you what to look for and where to look, in your own space, hands free. It's demonstration only, not form assessment, and our learning benefit is still a hypothesis we're testing. Thanks for watching." |
