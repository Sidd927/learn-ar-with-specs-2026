# Storylines considered (built: A)

**A — "Where to look" (built).** Device: the result first, then the system. Form: one home stage (the real preview) with an editorial column; a single push from the Lens Studio window into the preview establishes that it's real.
First 2 s: the Knees comparison (red over white) and "Watch the movement."
Beats: hook (the comparison) → establish (Lens Studio → preview, title) → demonstration (five phases, stepping) → compare (Knees, Heels, Chest, each from its view, each returned) → explore (angles, half speed) → replay → why head-worn → what's next → end card.
Look: deep charcoal ground, the product's own teal as the one accent, Inter. Sound: narration, clicks on real presses, two whooshes, no music.
Why picked: judges see the real product's central idea in the first frame, then every claim is shown happening in the real UI.

**B — "One question, three answers."** Each comparison is framed as a question title card ("Do the knees track over the toes?") answered by the footage. Not built: question cards over a product that doesn't assess anyone risk implying feedback on the viewer.

**C — "Continuous take."** No cuts: one camera move through the Lens Studio window into each comparison. Not built: the footage is a 5-minute take with long idle holds. A one-take film would either show the waiting or need speed ramps that misrepresent the pace of the Lens.
